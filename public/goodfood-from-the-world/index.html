The Program

How it works

01
📝

Subscribers Submit

Any subscriber submits their original recipe with their story, cultural background, and inspiration. A simple portal makes it effortless — in minutes, not hours.

02
🗳️

Community Votes

The community votes on their favourites weekly. Every vote is a touchpoint between boxes — building engagement, anticipation, and a reason to stay subscribed.

03
🏆

Top 3 Featured

The three highest-voted recipes are tested, refined, and featured in the weekly RTE lineup — with the creator's face, name, and story printed on every box.

04
💰

Royalty Per Box

For every box sold, the creator earns a per-box royalty. Real income, real recognition — transforming a cherished recipe into a source of pride and earnings.

The Long Game

How GoodFood
changed their lives

The program doesn't end when the box ships. Over time, GoodFood will run a series of creator campaigns — real, unscripted stories of how the program transformed the lives of the people who started as subscribers and became known worldwide. These campaigns become the most powerful marketing asset GoodFood has ever built.

I
Phase 1 · Launch

"Meet the Creator" Series

Short-form video profiles of the first cohort of creators. Released on social media as the program launches — building awareness, curiosity, and emotional pull. Subscribers see themselves in the creators.

II
Phase 2 · 6 Months

"What Happened Next" Follow-Ups

Return to creators 6 months later. How many boxes sold? How much did they earn? Did their family know? Did strangers reach out? These stories write themselves — and the media will run them for free.

III
Phase 3 · Annual

"GoodFood Changed My Life" Campaign

Annual flagship campaign featuring the year's top creators. A Francesca who paid off debt. An Alex who started a catering business. A Nomvula who connected with her culture from across the ocean. Advertising that moves people to tears — and to subscribe.

The Economics

High signal.
Low cost.

The royalty cost is modest relative to what it unlocks. A creator who earns $4,000 in a single week doesn't just become a customer for life — they become our most credible salesperson.

The psychological value of earning real money from something you created far exceeds the dollar amount. The story of that earning spreads organically — in family WhatsApp groups, at dinner tables, on Instagram — reaching audiences no paid campaign ever could.

Compare this to a cost-per-acquisition through digital advertising. The creator's network often delivers at a fraction of the cost, with 10× the conversion trust of any banner or sponsored post.
