GoodFood Canada — Internal Strategy

AI Agents Strategy

One AI agent per major department: each reads its area, suggests best actions, and automates safe tasks. All agents work together so the company learns faster and executes consistently.

↓ Scroll for full strategy

What We’re Doing

In simple terms

We will build one AI agent for each major department at Goodfood.

Each agent will read what’s happening in its area, suggest best actions, and automate safe tasks.

All agents will work together so the company learns faster and executes consistently.

Why

  • Lower costs (customer service + waste)
  • Reduce refunds
  • Improve retention
  • Improve menu quality
  • Increase margin
→ Directly improving EBITDA.

How It Works — One Shared AI Foundation

All agents use the same:

Customer & order context

What was ordered, delivery issues, preferences, history.

Feedback & sentiment

Tickets, reviews, surveys, social comments.

Menu, inventory & costs

Ingredients, supplier constraints, recipe ratings.

Company rules

Brand tone, refund rules, food safety escalation, privacy rules.

This ensures agents are consistent, don’t contradict each other, and follow Goodfood policies.

The Department Agents

Customer Experience (Support + Feedback)

1 — Voice of Customer

Customer Feedback Agent

  • Reads feedback across reviews, surveys, support
  • Summarizes biggest issues each week (refunds, churn drivers)
  • Suggests fixes and priorities with estimated impact
2

Customer Service Agent

  • Helps agents answer faster with approved responses
  • Routes issues to the right team automatically
  • Suggests credits/refunds based on rules (approval when needed)
3

Social & Community Agent

  • Tracks what people say online, flags risks early
  • Suggests responses and content ideas
  • Alerts team if something is trending or a brand issue

Marketing & Growth

4

Retention & Loyalty Agent

  • Identifies customers at risk of leaving; suggests best outreach
  • Creates targeted win-back and loyalty messages (not blanket discounts)
  • Recommends weekly tests to improve retention and margin
5

Acquisition & Creative Agent

  • Produces ad angles, copy, creative briefs from what’s working
  • Keeps messaging on-brand, avoids risky claims
  • Speeds up marketing production cycles

Menu, Recipes & Operations

6

Recipe & Menu Curation Agent

  • Suggests weekly menus: taste + simplicity + cost + feasibility
  • Flags recipes that cause complaints (too long, bland, portions)
  • Improves personalization (quick meals, family, high-protein, etc.)
7

Inventory & Substitution Agent

  • Warns early if ingredients are at risk of shortage
  • Suggests better substitutions customers will accept
  • Helps reduce waste and spoilage
8 — High control

Quality & Food Safety Agent

  • Detects serious issues (allergens, illness reports, packaging breaches)
  • Escalates immediately to the right leaders
  • Provides approved templates and steps for fast, safe response

Finance / EBITDA

9

EBITDA & Performance Agent

  • Tracks where money is lost (refunds, credits, waste, inefficient promos)
  • Helps prioritize initiatives by ROI
  • Maintains an “EBITDA impact scoreboard” for leadership

How Agents Work Together

Shared signals → coordinated action. When one agent finds an issue, it notifies the others so everyone responds consistently.

Example: If the Feedback Agent sees “soggy greens” rising, the Recipe Agent reviews recipe/packaging, the Inventory Agent checks sourcing, and Customer Service uses an updated response.
Example: If the Social Agent sees a negative trend, it alerts Customer Service (for messaging) and Quality (if safety-related).
Example: If Retention sees churn rising around a specific recipe type, it feeds that back into Menu Curation.

Clear rules on what can be automated

Auto (safe)

Summarizing, tagging, drafting replies, routing, weekly reports, suggesting actions.

Approval required

Credits/refunds execution, menu changes, pricing changes, public statements, food safety communications.

Control stays where it matters; time is saved everywhere else.

The executive takeaway

This is not “AI for AI’s sake.” It’s a practical system where each department gets an AI assistant, and all assistants work together to:

  • Automate routine work
  • Recommend best practices
  • Prevent issues before they grow
  • Improve EBITDA with measurable results
