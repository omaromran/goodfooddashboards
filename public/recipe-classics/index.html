Proof It Works

HelloFresh (UK)

❌ No permanent classics

  • Hall of Fame recipes still rotate — not on demand
  • Cut 15% workforce, closed warehouse (900 jobs)
  • No pre-tax profit since 2020
  • ~9% 12-month retention

Gousto (UK) — "Everyday Favourites"

✅ Permanent classics, always on menu

  • Top 10 family classics never leave the menu
  • Cut losses by £55M in 2024
  • Record EBITDA £42M; positive free cashflow
  • Revenue +11% H1 2025 — "market-beating growth"
Same market. Gousto has permanent classics; HelloFresh doesn't — and cut 900 jobs. Classics aren't the only reason Gousto is winning, but they're a proven retention lever.
